PurityWallet is a self-custody wallet for Bitcoin Purity (BTC1), for creating or restoring wallets, receiving funds, and making payments from your device.

Create a standard wallet or import a supported wallet. Use watch-only and multisig setups to monitor funds or organize shared spending. Connect to an Electrum server, including the Bitcoin Purity network default, or configure your own.

WALLET FEATURES

• Create and restore a Bitcoin Purity wallet.
• Receive, send, and review Bitcoin Purity transactions.
• Choose transaction fees and manage UTXOs with coin control.
• Import watch-only and multisig wallets, and use supported hardware-wallet workflows.
• Connect to your own Electrum server.
• Use device security options to help protect access to your wallet.

No account or sign-in is required. Keep your recovery backup secure.

PurityWallet uses a separate app container from BlueWallet. Existing BlueWallet app data does not transfer automatically; back up your wallet and import it into PurityWallet.

Lightning wallet creation and Lightning network settings are not available in this release.
